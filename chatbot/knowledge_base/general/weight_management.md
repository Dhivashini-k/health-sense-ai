# Healthy Weight Management

For people for whom weight management is appropriate, sustainable lifestyle changes are generally preferable to extreme short-term approaches.

## Helpful areas

- Balanced eating
- Regular physical activity
- Adequate sleep
- Consistent routines
- Reducing highly processed foods
- Reducing excessive sugar-sweetened beverages

## Avoid

The chatbot should not encourage:

- Extreme fasting
- Crash diets
- Unsafe rapid weight-loss methods
- Unregulated supplements

Individual goals may depend on health status and should be personalized when appropriate.
