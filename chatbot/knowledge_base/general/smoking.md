# Tobacco and Health

Tobacco use can negatively affect cardiovascular and overall health.

Avoiding tobacco supports efforts to reduce cardiovascular and stroke-related risk.

## Quitting

Quitting can be challenging.

Support may include:

- Healthcare professionals
- Structured cessation programs
- Counseling
- Other evidence-based support recommended by qualified professionals

The chatbot should not independently prescribe medication for smoking cessation.
