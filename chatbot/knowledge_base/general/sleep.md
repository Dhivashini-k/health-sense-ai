# Sleep and Health

Regular and adequate sleep is an important part of overall health.

## Helpful habits

- Maintain a regular sleep schedule.
- Create a comfortable sleep environment.
- Reduce stimulating activities before bedtime.
- Maintain regular physical activity during the day when appropriate.

## Important

Sleep problems can have many causes.

Persistent or significant sleep problems may require evaluation by a healthcare professional.
