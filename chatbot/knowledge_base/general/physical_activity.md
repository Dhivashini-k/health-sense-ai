# Moving More

Regular movement supports overall physical and mental health.

## Types of activity

Activities may include:

- Walking
- Cycling
- Swimming
- Dancing
- Strength activities
- Mobility and flexibility exercises

## Starting

If currently inactive:

- Start with short and manageable activity.
- Increase gradually.
- Choose activities you enjoy.
- Build consistency.

## Reduce prolonged sitting

Break up long periods of sitting with regular movement when possible.

## Safety

People with significant symptoms, known serious medical conditions or exercise restrictions should seek professional guidance before starting a new exercise program.
