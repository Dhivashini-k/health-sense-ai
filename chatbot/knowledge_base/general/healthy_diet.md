# Healthy Eating

A healthy dietary pattern should be varied, balanced and sustainable.

## Include a variety of

- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Whole grains
- Beans and legumes
- Nuts and seeds where appropriate
- Suitable protein sources
- Minimally processed foods

## Consider limiting frequent intake of

- Highly processed foods
- Excess sodium
- Foods high in unhealthy fats
- Sugar-sweetened beverages
- Large amounts of foods high in added sugar

## Practical habits

- Drink water regularly.
- Include vegetables in major meals.
- Choose whole or minimally processed foods more often.
- Prepare meals at home when practical.
- Avoid extreme or restrictive diets unless medically advised.

## Personalisation

General dietary guidance should be adjusted when a person has:

- Kidney disease
- Diabetes
- Food allergies
- Medical dietary restrictions
- Other relevant health conditions
