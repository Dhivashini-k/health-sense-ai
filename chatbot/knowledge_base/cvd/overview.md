# Understanding Cardiovascular Risk

A cardiovascular risk result indicates that available screening information may suggest increased risk related to heart and blood vessel health.

A risk result is not a confirmed diagnosis of cardiovascular disease.

## Factors that can influence cardiovascular health

These may include:

- Blood pressure
- Physical inactivity
- Smoking
- Dietary patterns
- Body weight
- Blood sugar and metabolic health
- Family history
- Other clinical factors

## General prevention strategies

Support cardiovascular health through:

- A varied and heart-healthy diet
- Regular physical activity
- Avoiding tobacco
- Managing blood pressure and other risk factors
- Maintaining a healthy body weight where appropriate
- Attending recommended healthcare follow-up

## Important

The chatbot must not interpret a risk score as proof that a patient has heart disease.
