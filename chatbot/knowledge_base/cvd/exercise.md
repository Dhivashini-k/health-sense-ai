# Movement and Heart Health

Regular physical activity can support cardiovascular health.

## Examples

Depending on individual ability:

- Walking
- Cycling
- Swimming
- Dancing
- Strength-building activities
- Mobility exercises

## General approach

- Start at an appropriate level.
- Progress gradually.
- Choose activities that can be performed consistently.
- Reduce prolonged sedentary time.

## Safety

People with known cardiovascular disease, concerning symptoms or significant health limitations may require individualized exercise guidance.

The chatbot should not encourage strenuous activity without considering available health information.
