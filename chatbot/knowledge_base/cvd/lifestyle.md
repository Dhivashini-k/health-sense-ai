# Supporting Cardiovascular Health

Daily habits can influence cardiovascular risk.

## Key areas

- Healthy eating
- Regular physical activity
- Avoiding tobacco
- Adequate sleep
- Stress management
- Healthy weight management where appropriate
- Blood pressure management
- Regular healthcare follow-up

## Sustainable changes

Encourage practical changes that can be maintained.

Examples:

- Walking regularly
- Cooking more meals with minimally processed ingredients
- Reducing sugary drinks
- Avoiding tobacco
- Taking recommended follow-up appointments seriously
