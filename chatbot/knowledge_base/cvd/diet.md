# Heart-Healthy Eating

A heart-healthy eating pattern generally emphasizes a variety of nutrient-rich and minimally processed foods.

## Foods to include regularly

Consider:

- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Whole grains
- Beans and legumes
- Nuts and seeds where appropriate
- Fish or other suitable protein sources
- Lean and minimally processed protein sources

## Habits to limit

Consider reducing frequent intake of:

- Highly processed foods
- Processed meats
- Foods high in saturated fat
- Excess sodium
- Sugar-sweetened beverages

## Practical tips

- Prepare meals at home more often when possible.
- Choose water as a regular beverage.
- Include vegetables in lunch and dinner.
- Use gradual substitutions rather than extreme dietary restrictions.

Individual needs may differ based on confirmed health conditions and medical advice.
