# Kidney Health and Diet

Dietary needs can vary significantly between individuals with kidney disease.

## For elevated kidney-related risk without confirmed CKD

General health guidance may include:

- Choosing minimally processed foods more often
- Avoiding excessive sodium
- Supporting healthy blood pressure
- Supporting healthy blood sugar
- Following a balanced dietary pattern

## Important caution

Do not automatically recommend strict restrictions on:

- Potassium
- Phosphorus
- Protein
- Fluids

These requirements may depend on:

- Kidney function
- Disease stage
- Laboratory results
- Medications
- Dialysis status
- Healthcare professional recommendations

## Hydration

Do not prescribe a specific fluid intake for people with known kidney disease unless this information is part of an approved care plan.

General healthy adults may benefit from regular hydration, but individual fluid needs vary.

## Safety

For confirmed kidney disease, individualized nutrition guidance may be necessary.
