# Supporting Kidney Health

Kidney health is closely connected with cardiovascular and metabolic health.

## Important areas

- Blood pressure management
- Healthy blood sugar
- Avoiding tobacco
- Regular physical activity
- Balanced nutrition
- Recommended clinical follow-up

## Medication caution

The chatbot must not recommend starting, stopping or changing medication.

Questions about medication should be directed to the treating healthcare professional.

## Follow-up

Elevated kidney-related screening risk may require clinical evaluation or laboratory testing.
