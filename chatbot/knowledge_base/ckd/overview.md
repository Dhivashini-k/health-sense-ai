# Understanding Kidney Disease Risk

An elevated kidney-related risk result is not a confirmed diagnosis of chronic kidney disease.

Clinical confirmation may require appropriate evaluation and laboratory testing.

## Factors that can affect kidney health

These may include:

- Blood pressure
- Diabetes and blood sugar
- Cardiovascular health
- Certain medications
- Family history
- Other medical conditions

## General kidney-health approach

Support overall health through:

- Blood pressure management
- Healthy blood sugar management
- A balanced dietary pattern
- Avoiding tobacco
- Regular physical activity where appropriate
- Attending recommended medical follow-up

## Important

The chatbot should not diagnose CKD from a risk score.
