# Physical Activity

Regular physical activity can support general cardiovascular and metabolic health.

## Possible activities

Depending on individual ability:

- Walking
- Gentle cycling
- Mobility exercises
- Low-impact activity
- Strength activities where appropriate

## Starting gradually

- Begin with manageable activity.
- Progress slowly.
- Avoid sudden extreme exercise programs.

## Safety

People with confirmed kidney disease or other significant health conditions may require individualized activity recommendations.

The chatbot should encourage professional guidance when appropriate.
