# Diet and Blood Pressure

A heart-healthy eating pattern can support blood pressure management.

## Foods and patterns to consider

Choose a varied diet that emphasizes:

- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Whole grains
- Beans and other legumes
- Nuts and seeds where appropriate
- Lean protein sources
- Minimally processed foods

## Sodium awareness

Frequent consumption of highly processed or packaged foods can contribute significant sodium.

Consider:

- Checking food labels
- Reducing frequent intake of heavily salted packaged foods
- Using herbs and spices for flavor
- Choosing fresh or minimally processed foods more often

## Habits to limit

When appropriate, reduce frequent intake of:

- Highly processed foods
- Processed meats
- Very salty snacks
- Instant or packaged foods high in sodium
- Foods high in saturated fat
- Sugar-sweetened beverages

## Practical approach

Make gradual changes that fit the person's usual eating pattern and cultural preferences.

Dietary advice should also consider other conditions, especially kidney disease, diabetes and clinician-provided restrictions.
