# Lifestyle and Blood Pressure

Multiple lifestyle factors can influence cardiovascular health.

## Areas to focus on

- Healthy eating patterns
- Regular physical activity
- Reducing excessive sodium intake
- Maintaining a healthy body weight where appropriate
- Avoiding tobacco
- Getting adequate sleep
- Managing stress
- Attending recommended follow-up

## Monitoring

If a healthcare professional recommends blood pressure monitoring, follow the method and schedule they provide.

The chatbot should not independently change medication or treatment based on a blood pressure reading.
