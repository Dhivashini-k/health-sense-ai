# Understanding Blood Pressure Risk

Health Sense AI may identify elevated risk related to blood pressure using available screening information.

A screening result is not a confirmed diagnosis of hypertension.

A diagnosis of hypertension requires appropriate blood pressure measurement and clinical evaluation.

## Why blood pressure matters

Persistently elevated blood pressure can increase the risk of:

- Cardiovascular disease
- Stroke
- Kidney problems
- Other health complications

## General risk-reduction strategies

Lifestyle measures that may support healthy blood pressure include:

- Following a healthy dietary pattern
- Reducing excessive sodium intake
- Staying physically active
- Maintaining a healthy body weight where appropriate
- Avoiding tobacco
- Managing stress
- Attending recommended healthcare follow-up

## Important language

Use:

"Your screening identified elevated blood-pressure-related risk."

Do not say:

"You definitely have hypertension."

Clinical confirmation is required.
