# Eating Patterns for Metabolic Health

A balanced dietary pattern may support healthy blood sugar and overall metabolic health.

## Foods and patterns to consider

Choose a variety of:

- Vegetables
- Whole fruits
- Whole grains
- Beans and other legumes
- Nuts and seeds where appropriate
- Lean or minimally processed protein sources
- Minimally processed foods

## Practical meal structure

A simple balanced meal can include:

- A large portion of non-starchy vegetables
- A source of protein
- A moderate portion of carbohydrate-rich food
- Healthy fats where appropriate

## Habits to limit

Consider reducing frequent consumption of:

- Sugar-sweetened beverages
- Highly processed snacks
- Large portions of refined carbohydrate foods
- Frequent desserts high in added sugar
- Highly processed foods with little fiber

## Practical tips

- Prefer water over sugary drinks.
- Include fiber-rich foods regularly.
- Combine carbohydrate-containing foods with protein or fiber where appropriate.
- Pay attention to portion sizes.
- Avoid extreme diets unless recommended by a qualified professional.
- Make gradual and sustainable changes.

## Personalisation

Dietary needs may vary based on:

- Confirmed medical conditions
- Kidney health
- Medications
- Allergies
- Cultural food preferences
- Activity level

General educational guidance should not replace an individualized medical nutrition plan.
