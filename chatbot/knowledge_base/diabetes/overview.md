# Understanding Diabetes Risk

Health Sense AI may identify an elevated risk related to diabetes based on the available screening information.

An elevated risk result is not the same as a confirmed diagnosis. A diagnosis requires appropriate clinical evaluation and testing by a qualified healthcare professional.

## Factors commonly associated with type 2 diabetes risk

Factors may include:

- Higher body weight or excess body fat
- Low levels of physical activity
- Unhealthy dietary patterns
- Family history
- Increasing age
- Elevated blood pressure
- Other metabolic risk factors

## General risk-reduction approach

General lifestyle measures that may support metabolic health include:

- Eating a varied and balanced diet
- Choosing minimally processed foods more often
- Including vegetables, legumes and whole grains where appropriate
- Limiting sugar-sweetened beverages
- Staying physically active
- Reducing prolonged sitting
- Maintaining a healthy body weight where appropriate
- Avoiding tobacco
- Attending recommended health screenings

## Important note

The chatbot must not say that a person definitely has diabetes based only on a risk score.

Use language such as:

"Your screening identified an elevated diabetes-related risk. A healthcare professional may recommend appropriate follow-up or testing."

Do not say:

"You have diabetes."
