# Eating for Brain and Cardiovascular Health

A heart-healthy dietary pattern can support factors associated with stroke prevention.

## Emphasize

- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Whole grains
- Beans and legumes
- Minimally processed foods
- Appropriate protein sources
- Healthy fats where appropriate

## Limit frequent intake of

- Highly processed foods
- Excess sodium
- Processed meats
- Sugar-sweetened beverages
- Foods high in unhealthy fats

## Practical approach

Focus on improving overall dietary patterns rather than relying on a single "stroke prevention food."

Dietary recommendations should be considered alongside blood pressure, cardiovascular health, diabetes risk and any clinician-provided care plan.
