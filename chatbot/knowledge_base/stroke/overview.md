# Understanding Stroke Risk

A stroke risk result estimates risk based on available information.

It does not mean that the person currently has a stroke.

## Factors associated with stroke risk

Factors may include:

- Elevated blood pressure
- Cardiovascular health
- Smoking
- Diabetes and metabolic health
- Physical inactivity
- Dietary patterns
- Other clinical factors

## General prevention

Risk reduction may involve:

- Managing blood pressure
- Following a heart-healthy dietary pattern
- Staying physically active
- Avoiding tobacco
- Managing other identified health risks
- Attending appropriate healthcare follow-up

## Important

The chatbot must not say:

"You have had a stroke."

or:

"You are going to have a stroke."

Use risk-focused language instead.
