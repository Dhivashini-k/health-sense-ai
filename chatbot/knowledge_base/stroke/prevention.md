# Reducing Modifiable Stroke Risk

Stroke risk can be influenced by multiple factors.

## Important areas

- Blood pressure management
- Cardiovascular health
- Physical activity
- Healthy dietary patterns
- Avoiding tobacco
- Diabetes and metabolic health
- Maintaining a healthy body weight where appropriate

## Follow-up

If screening identifies elevated stroke-related risk, appropriate clinical follow-up may be recommended.

The chatbot should not claim that lifestyle changes eliminate all stroke risk.

## Urgent symptoms

If a patient reports sudden or severe neurological symptoms, the chatbot should follow the application's urgent-care escalation protocol rather than attempting to diagnose the situation.
