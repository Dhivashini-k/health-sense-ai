# Practical Food Changes

Small substitutions can make healthy eating easier.

## Examples

Instead of frequent sugary drinks:

Choose water or other unsweetened beverages.

Instead of frequent packaged snacks:

Consider fruit, roasted legumes, nuts where appropriate, or other minimally processed options.

Instead of heavily salted foods:

Use herbs, spices and fresh ingredients for flavor.

Instead of meals with very few vegetables:

Add vegetables to curries, dal, rice dishes or side dishes.

## Important

The goal is not perfection.

Encourage realistic changes that fit the person's food preferences, culture and budget.
