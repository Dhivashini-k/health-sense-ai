# Healthy Indian Meal Patterns

Traditional Indian meals can support a balanced dietary pattern.

## Breakfast ideas

Depending on individual needs:

- Idli with sambar
- Vegetable upma
- Vegetable poha
- Oats with suitable additions
- Dosa with vegetable-based accompaniments
- Other balanced regional meals

## Main meals

A balanced meal may include:

- Vegetables
- Dal, beans or another protein source
- Rice, roti or another grain in an appropriate portion
- Curd or another suitable food when appropriate

## Practical principle

Focus on:

- More vegetables
- Adequate protein
- Appropriate portions
- Whole or minimally processed foods

No single Indian food is universally "good" or "bad."

Portion size, preparation method and the individual's health profile matter.
