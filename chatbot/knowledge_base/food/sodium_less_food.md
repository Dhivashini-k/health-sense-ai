# Reducing Excess Sodium

Some commonly consumed foods may contain significant amounts of sodium.

## Foods to consume less frequently when high in sodium

Depending on preparation:

- Pickles
- Papad
- Packaged namkeen
- Instant noodles
- Highly processed snacks
- Processed or preserved foods
- Some packaged sauces and mixes

## Practical alternatives

- Use herbs and spices for flavor.
- Prepare fresh food more often.
- Check packaged food labels.
- Gradually reduce added salt.

## Important

Do not recommend eliminating salt completely unless directed by a healthcare professional.
