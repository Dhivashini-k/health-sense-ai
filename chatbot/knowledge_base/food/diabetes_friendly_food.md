# Indian Meals and Metabolic Health

A diabetes-supportive eating pattern does not require eliminating all rice, roti or traditional foods.

## Helpful principles

- Include vegetables regularly.
- Include protein sources such as dal, beans or other suitable foods.
- Pay attention to portions of carbohydrate-rich foods.
- Prefer higher-fiber options when practical.
- Limit frequent sugary drinks and heavily processed snacks.

## Examples

Depending on individual needs:

- Dal with vegetables and an appropriate portion of rice or roti
- Vegetable-based meals with a suitable protein source
- Whole grains or millets where appropriate
- Traditional foods prepared with less added sugar and excessive fat

## Important

No single food automatically prevents or causes diabetes.

Overall dietary pattern and individual context matter.
